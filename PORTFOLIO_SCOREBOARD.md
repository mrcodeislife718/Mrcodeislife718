## Immediate portfolio priorities

1. **Finish & monetize now:** Sessions, JobFlow, Measure, WorkForce, Jiffy Laundry, Muze, Watchable, Codeable.
2. **Produce hard engineering proof:** applied-ai-erp-agent, Sessions, Measure, OverDrive/Prism/VaultRam/Focus/Maestro, Hired-Ai, JobFlow.
3. **Research proof tranche:** Einstein, OverDrive stack, Epiphany, ECA-1, G.A.I.A, Bio-Gene.
4. **Platform ecosystem tranche:** Scout/Cannon/Nova/Parallel/Cadence/Sprout/Velocity/Chronos/Syncio/Plasma/Cortex.
5. **Incubate strategically:** remaining commercial/media/proof assets until a dependency, market event, or high-information experiment promotes them.

## Rule for upgrades

A cell is upgraded only when its project-level `PORTFOLIO_PROOF.md` points to the exact command, artifact, benchmark result, deployment URL/evidence, usage record, commercial record, incumbent comparison, customer-switching evidence, moat evidence, or acquisition result that proves it. This scoreboard is intentionally conservative.

`PORTFOLIO_VERTICAL_DISRUPTION_MATRIX.md` is the operating record for the market side of the portfolio. The scoreboard records state; the matrix records what must happen next.
